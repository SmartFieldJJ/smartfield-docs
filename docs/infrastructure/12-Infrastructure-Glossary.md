# Infrastructure Glossary

## Purpose

This glossary defines the infrastructure-related terminology used throughout the SmartField architecture documentation.

Its purpose is to establish a common vocabulary for technical concepts related to infrastructure, deployment, integrations, and operational capabilities.

---

# Glossary

## Adapter

A concrete implementation that connects the application to an external technology or service while implementing a defined architectural contract.

---

## Authentication

The process of verifying the identity of a user or system before granting access to application resources.

---

## Availability

The ability of a system to remain operational and accessible when required.

---

## Configuration

The collection of runtime parameters that control application behavior without modifying source code.

---

## Dependency

An external component, service, or resource required by an application to perform its functions.

---

## Deployment

The process of packaging, releasing, and executing application components within a runtime environment.

---

## External Service

A system outside the application boundary that communicates with SmartField through defined integration contracts.

---

## Fault Isolation

The architectural practice of containing failures within a limited scope to prevent them from affecting unrelated components.

---

## Health Check

A mechanism used to determine whether an application or infrastructure component is operating correctly.

---

## Identity Provider

A service responsible for authenticating identities and establishing trusted security information.

---

## Infrastructure Adapter

A component that implements a port defined by the Application or Domain layer using a specific technology.

---

## Logging

The recording of operational events to support diagnostics, monitoring, and troubleshooting.

---

## Message

A unit of information exchanged between application components or external systems.

---

## Metrics

Quantitative measurements that describe the operational behavior and performance of an application.

---

## Observability

The capability to understand the internal state of a system through logs, metrics, traces, and other operational telemetry.

---

## Persistence

The technical capability responsible for storing and retrieving business information while preserving architectural boundaries.

---

## Port

An architectural contract that defines how higher-level layers communicate with infrastructure implementations.

---

## Resilience

The ability of a system to tolerate failures, recover from disruptions, and continue operating under adverse conditions.

---

## Retry

A resilience mechanism that repeats a failed operation when recovery is considered possible.

---

## Secret

Sensitive configuration data that must be protected from unauthorized access, such as credentials, certificates, or encryption keys.

---

## Security Context

The authenticated identity and associated security information available during request processing.

---

## Storage

The capability to manage files, documents, media, and other non-transactional resources independently of business persistence.

---

## Telemetry

Operational data generated by the application, including logs, metrics, and traces, used for monitoring and diagnostics.

---

## Trace

A record of the execution path followed by a request as it traverses application components.

---

## Transaction

A unit of work executed atomically to ensure consistent modification of persistent business data.
