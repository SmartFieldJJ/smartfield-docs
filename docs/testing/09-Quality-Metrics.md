\# Quality Metrics



\## Purpose



This document defines the quality metrics strategy adopted by SmartField.



Quality metrics provide objective information about the effectiveness of the testing process and the overall quality of the software, supporting continuous improvement and informed decision-making throughout the software lifecycle.



Metrics should guide improvement efforts rather than serve as goals by themselves.



\---



\# Scope



This document defines:



\- Quality measurement objectives.

\- Quality indicators.

\- Metric categories.

\- Measurement principles.

\- Continuous improvement.



It does not define:



\- Project-specific quality targets.

\- Team performance evaluation.

\- Operational monitoring.

\- Production observability.

\- Business KPIs.



These concerns are documented in their respective architectural documents.



\---



\# Quality Measurement Objectives



Quality metrics aim to:



\- Evaluate testing effectiveness.

\- Improve software quality.

\- Detect quality trends.

\- Support continuous improvement.

\- Increase confidence in software releases.



Metrics should provide meaningful information that supports engineering decisions.



\---



\# Metric Categories



Quality may be evaluated through multiple categories of metrics.



Typical categories include:



\- Test coverage.

\- Test execution.

\- Defect detection.

\- Test stability.

\- Test maintainability.

\- Release quality.



No single metric is sufficient to evaluate overall software quality.



\---



\# Verification Scope



Quality metrics may provide insight into:



\- Testing completeness.

\- Regression detection.

\- Defect trends.

\- Test reliability.

\- Verification effectiveness.

\- Software evolution.



Metrics should be interpreted collectively rather than individually.



\---



\# Metric Characteristics



Useful quality metrics should be:



\- Objective.

\- Repeatable.

\- Understandable.

\- Actionable.

\- Consistent.

\- Relevant.



Metrics that do not support improvement should be reconsidered.



\---



\# Continuous Improvement



Quality metrics should support continuous improvement by identifying opportunities to strengthen both the software and the testing process.



Measurement should encourage:



\- Better testing practices.

\- Improved software reliability.

\- Reduced defect recurrence.

\- More effective verification.

\- Sustainable quality growth.



The objective is continuous learning rather than metric optimization.



\---



\# Quality Measurement Principles



Quality metrics should:



\- Support engineering decisions.

\- Reflect meaningful quality characteristics.

\- Avoid encouraging undesirable behaviors.

\- Be interpreted within their context.

\- Complement engineering judgment.



Metrics are indicators of quality, not quality itself.



\---



\# Quality Metrics vs Test Automation



| Quality Metrics | Test Automation |

|-----------------|-----------------|

| Measures testing effectiveness | Executes tests automatically |

| Provides quality indicators | Provides continuous verification |

| Supports improvement decisions | Supports execution efficiency |

| Evaluates testing outcomes | Evaluates software behavior |



Automation generates information that quality metrics help interpret.
