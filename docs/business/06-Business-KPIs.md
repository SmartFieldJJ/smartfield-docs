\# Business KPIs



\## Purpose



This document defines the Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) used to measure the effectiveness of SmartField from a business perspective.



KPIs provide objective metrics that help organizations evaluate operational performance, identify improvement opportunities, and monitor business outcomes over time.



These indicators measure business success rather than technical system performance.



\---



\# Scope



This document defines:



\- Business performance indicators

\- Measurement objectives

\- Calculation methods

\- Monitoring frequency



It does not define:



\- Infrastructure monitoring

\- Application metrics

\- Performance benchmarks

\- Technical observability



These topics are documented in the Infrastructure and DevOps sections.



\---



\# KPI Overview



| KPI | Business Area | Objective |

|-----|---------------|-----------|

| Active Companies | Company Management | Measure platform adoption. |

| Active Employees | Workforce Management | Monitor workforce participation. |

| Active Projects | Project Management | Measure operational activity. |

| Assignment Coverage | Assignment Management | Evaluate workforce allocation. |

| Project Completion Rate | Project Management | Measure project execution success. |

| Employee Assignment Rate | Workforce Management | Evaluate employee utilization. |

| Authentication Success Rate | Identity \& Access Management | Measure secure access reliability. |

| Audit Coverage | Audit \& Traceability | Verify traceability of business operations. |

\# Company Management KPIs



\## Active Companies



\### Objective



Measure the number of organizations actively using SmartField.



\### Formula



```

Number of Active Companies

```



\### Frequency



Monthly



\---



\# Workforce Management KPIs



\## Active Employees



\### Objective



Measure the number of employees currently available for operational work.



\### Formula



```

Employees with Active Status

```



\### Frequency



Daily



\---



\## Employee Assignment Rate



\### Objective



Measure how effectively employees are assigned to operational projects.



\### Formula



```

Assigned Employees

\---------------------------

Active Employees

```



\### Frequency



Weekly



\---



\# Project Management KPIs



\## Active Projects



\### Objective



Measure the number of operational projects currently in progress.



\### Formula



```

Projects with Active Status

```



\### Frequency



Weekly



\---



\## Project Completion Rate



\### Objective



Measure the percentage of completed projects.



\### Formula



```

Completed Projects

\-----------------------------

Total Projects

```



\### Frequency



Monthly

\# Assignment Management KPIs



\## Assignment Coverage



\### Objective



Measure the percentage of projects that have assigned personnel.



\### Formula



```

Projects with Assignments

\--------------------------------

Total Active Projects

```



\### Frequency



Weekly



\---



\# Identity \& Access Management KPIs



\## Authentication Success Rate



\### Objective



Measure the percentage of successful authentication attempts.



\### Formula



```

Successful Logins

\----------------------------

Total Login Attempts

```



\### Frequency



Daily



\---



\# Audit \& Traceability KPIs



\## Audit Coverage



\### Objective



Measure how many critical business operations generate audit records.



\### Formula



```

Audited Business Events

\-----------------------------------

Critical Business Events

```



\### Frequency



Monthly

\# KPI Governance



Business KPIs should follow these principles:



\- Every KPI must support a business objective.

\- KPIs should be measurable using reliable business data.

\- KPI definitions must remain stable over time.

\- Business owners are responsible for interpreting KPI results.

\- KPIs should be reviewed periodically to ensure continued relevance.
