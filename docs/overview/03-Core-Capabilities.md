\# Core Business Capabilities



\## Purpose



This document defines the core business capabilities provided by SmartField.



Business capabilities describe \*\*what the platform is able to do from a business perspective\*\*, independently of the underlying technical implementation.



They represent long-term organizational capabilities that remain stable as the system evolves.



\---



\# Scope



This document focuses exclusively on business capabilities.



It does not describe:



\- Use Cases

\- User Interfaces

\- REST APIs

\- Database entities

\- Technical architecture



Those topics are documented in their respective sections.



\---



\# Capability Model



SmartField is organized around the following core business capabilities.



```

SmartField



├── Identity \& Access Management

├── Company Management

├── Workforce Management

├── Project Management

├── Assignment Management

├── Mobile Operations

└── Audit \& Traceability

```



\---



\# Identity \& Access Management



\## Description



Provides secure authentication and authorization for every platform user.



\## Responsibilities



\- User authentication

\- User authorization

\- Role management

\- Permission management

\- Session management

\- Password management



\## Business Value



Ensures that only authorized users can access business information.



\---



\# Company Management



\## Description



Manages organizations registered within SmartField.



\## Responsibilities



\- Register companies

\- Maintain company information

\- Configure organization settings

\- Manage company lifecycle



\## Business Value



Allows multiple organizations to operate independently within the platform.



\---



\# Workforce Management



\## Description



Manages employees working within an organization.



\## Responsibilities



\- Employee registration

\- Employee profile management

\- Employment status

\- Supervisor relationships



\## Business Value



Provides centralized workforce administration.



\---



\# Project Management



\## Description



Organizes operational work into projects.



\## Responsibilities



\- Create projects

\- Update project information

\- Monitor project lifecycle

\- Associate supervisors



\## Business Value



Provides structure for operational activities.



\---



\# Assignment Management



\## Description



Connects employees with projects.



\## Responsibilities



\- Assign employees

\- Remove assignments

\- Manage participation

\- Track assignments



\## Business Value



Ensures that field personnel participate in the correct operational activities.



\---



\# Mobile Operations



\## Description



Supports field personnel through the mobile application.



\## Responsibilities



\- Mobile authentication

\- View assigned projects

\- Access personal information

\- Secure mobile sessions



\## Business Value



Allows employees to interact with SmartField from anywhere.



\---



\# Audit \& Traceability



\## Description



Maintains historical records of important business operations.



\## Responsibilities



\- Record critical events

\- Audit business operations

\- Support operational traceability

\- Facilitate compliance



\## Business Value



Provides accountability and operational transparency.



\---



\# Capability Relationships



The capabilities are interconnected.



Identity \& Access Management enables secure access to all other capabilities.



Company Management owns organizational information.



Workforce Management depends on Company Management.



Project Management depends on Company Management.



Assignment Management depends on both Workforce Management and Project Management.



Mobile Operations consumes business information generated by the previous capabilities.



Audit \& Traceability records relevant events across the entire platform.



\---



\# Capability Evolution



Future versions of SmartField may introduce additional capabilities such as:



\- Task Management

\- Scheduling

\- Asset Management

\- Inventory Management

\- Work Orders

\- Analytics

\- Artificial Intelligence

\- External Integrations



The existing capabilities should evolve without breaking their business responsibilities.
