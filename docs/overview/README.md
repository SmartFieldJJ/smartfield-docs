\# Overview



\## Purpose



The \*\*Overview\*\* section provides the high-level understanding of SmartField from a business and product perspective.



Its purpose is to introduce the platform before entering technical or architectural details, allowing stakeholders, developers, and AI assistants to quickly understand what SmartField is, why it exists, who uses it, and which business capabilities it provides.



This documentation represents the entry point to the project.



\---



\# Objectives



The documents contained in this section aim to:



\- Introduce SmartField and its purpose.

\- Define the product vision.

\- Establish a common business vocabulary.

\- Identify the actors interacting with the platform.

\- Describe the main business capabilities.

\- Present the business rules that guide the product.

\- Provide traceability to higher-level business objectives.



\---



\# Scope



The Overview documentation covers only business and product concepts.



It does \*\*not\*\* define:



\- System architecture

\- Technical implementation

\- Domain model

\- Application workflows

\- Infrastructure

\- APIs



Those topics are documented in their corresponding sections.



\---



\# Audience



This documentation is intended for:



\- Product Owners
